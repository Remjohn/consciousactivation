# Human Interview Question Grammar Library

## Constitutional rule
The objective is not to make the Guest say the answer we expect. The objective is to expose enough space for the Guest's actual map to appear.

## Q01 — Broad activation
Purpose: invite the Guest to enter the broad pressure selected by Phase 4.
Pattern: "When you look at [broad signal], what immediately comes to mind from your own experience?"

## Q02 — Specificity probe
Purpose: move from abstraction to a concrete scene, decision, or event.
Pattern: "Can you take me to a specific moment when that became real for you?"

## Q03 — Mechanism probe
Purpose: identify what actually caused, sustained, or changed the condition.
Pattern: "What do you think was actually happening underneath that?"

## Q04 — Contradiction probe
Purpose: surface tension without supplying its resolution.
Pattern: "What was true at the same time that seemed to contradict the obvious story?"

## Q05 — Cost / stakes probe
Purpose: expose what the issue actually cost or threatened.
Pattern: "What did this cost you that people from the outside probably wouldn't notice?"

## Q06 — Agency probe
Purpose: understand attribution without imposing blame.
Pattern: "At the time, what did you think was actually causing this?"

## Q07 — Belief probe
Purpose: identify a belief that structured behavior.
Pattern: "What did you believe then that you don't believe in the same way now?"

## Q08 — Counterexample probe
Purpose: stress-test a candidate interpretation.
Pattern: "Was there a time when that explanation didn't fit? What happened?"

## Q09 — Meaning probe
Purpose: elicit the Guest's own interpretation of the experience.
Pattern: "Looking back, what do you think that experience taught you that you couldn't have learned any other way?"

## Q10 — Language capture
Purpose: preserve signature wording.
Pattern: "What would you call that in your own words?"

## Q11 — Audience bridge
Purpose: connect authenticated Guest experience to audience reality without inventing equivalence.
Pattern: "Where do you see people you work with encountering something similar — and where are they actually different from you?"

## Q12 — Follow-up from evidence
Purpose: recurse on an unexpected high-value response.
Pattern: generated from the immediately preceding evidence state; must cite the evidence span it is following.

## Prohibited question construction
- questions whose grammar presupposes the Guest agrees with the Phase 4 hypothesis
- questions that contain the intended conclusion as a premise
- questions that replace concrete experience with marketing terminology
- questions that force false binaries when the evidence is ambiguous
- questions whose only purpose is to obtain a predetermined quote

## Escalation condition
If the next question cannot be generated without assuming an unverified claim, pause semantic inference and ask a neutral specificity or clarification question instead.
