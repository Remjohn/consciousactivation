# Phase 5 Anti-Centroid Patrol

## Mission
Protect human specificity throughout interview planning, capture, distillation, and semantic packaging. The patrol exists to detect statistical-centroid drift, not to make output more cautious.

## Failure classes

- `RLHF_CENTROID_SOFTENING`
- `PREMATURE_SANITIZATION`
- `GENERIC_PARAPHRASE`
- `CORPORATE_LANGUAGE_INJECTION`
- `CONVICTION_DILUTION`
- `CONTRADICTION_ERASURE`
- `EMOTIONAL_FLATTENING`
- `TRIBE_LANGUAGE_ERASURE`
- `EDGE_DILUTION`
- `FALSE_CONSENSUS`
- `OVER_EXPLANATION`

## Patrol questions
1. Did any distinctive Guest phrase disappear without evidence-based reason?
2. Did the system turn a contradiction into a harmonious summary?
3. Did a first-person statement become a generic audience insight?
4. Did a strong position get softened into a neutral corporate formulation?
5. Did a difficult but meaningful expression get replaced because it sounded unusual?
6. Did the abstraction remove the exact reason the evidence mattered?

## Positive anti-centroid rule
The patrol must preserve specificity while still allowing factual clarification, compression, and structure.

## Escalation
A high-severity centroid failure requires either restoration from source evidence or a human review of the derived artifact. The system may not solve centroid drift by inventing a stronger opposite extreme.
