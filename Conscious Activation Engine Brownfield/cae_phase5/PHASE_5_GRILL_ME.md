# Phase 5 Grill-Me Protocol

## Purpose
Interrogate the Human Interview, Authentication & Evidence Acquisition design one decision at a time until the architecture is materially shared, brownfield-grounded, and implementation-ready.

## Rules
1. Ask one question at a time.
2. Explore the codebase before asking anything the codebase can answer.
3. Every recommended answer must be substantive and grounded in Phase 5 source material and inspected brownfield reality.
4. Every recommendation must surface at least one architectural collision.
5. Never treat a phase hypothesis as Guest truth.
6. Never use generic safety prose as a substitute for a real architectural boundary.
7. Distinguish evidence, interpretation, state, and hypothesis in every recommendation.
8. Preserve contradictions until the evidence justifies resolution.
9. Test every decision against the Human-First contract and Anti-Centroid law.
10. Stop asking questions once the decision graph is sufficiently constrained for implementation.

## Required recommendation structure

- Recommended decision
- Why it follows from current CAE architecture
- Brownfield evidence to inspect
- Architectural collision surfaced
- Alternatives rejected
- Data-model consequence
- Runtime consequence
- Validator consequence
- Open dependency

## Signal-distillation law
Use the existing project's RSCS laws: saturate before compression, detect collisions, compress only after evidence, and evaluate against reality. The interview system itself is subject to those laws.
