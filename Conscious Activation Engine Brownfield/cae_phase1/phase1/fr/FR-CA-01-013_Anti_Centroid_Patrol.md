# FR-CA-01-013 — Anti-Centroid Patrol and Density Preservation

## Requirement
The engine SHALL maintain a diagnostic quality function dedicated to detecting generic mean-reversion and unjustified semantic flattening.

## Detection Targets
- generic abstractions replacing source specifics
- unnecessary hedging
- corporate neutrality
- irrelevant permission/authorization language
- flattening of contradiction
- loss of native vocabulary
- reduction of edge to safe topic summaries

## Operating Law
The patrol protects meaningful tension; it does not censor legitimate divergence.

## Outcomes
`PASS | WARN | REPAIR | ESCALATE | BLOCK`

BLOCK is reserved for an explicit contract or constitutional violation.
