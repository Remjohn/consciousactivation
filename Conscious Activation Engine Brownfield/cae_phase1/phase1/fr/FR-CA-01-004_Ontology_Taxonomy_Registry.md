# FR-CA-01-004 — Ontology and Taxonomy Registry

## Requirement
The engine SHALL maintain explicit parent/child, sibling, crosswalk, and boundary relationships for canonical taxonomies.

## Principle
Ontology answers what exists and what kind of thing it is. Taxonomy organizes those things. Neither is merely a list of database tables.

## Acceptance Criteria
- Each taxonomy node has a defined parent or documented root status.
- Taxonomic membership is not inferred from naming conventions alone.
- Ontology duplicates are detectable.
- Cross-plane concepts are represented through explicit mappings.
- Experimental concepts remain outside canonical taxonomy until promotion criteria are met.
