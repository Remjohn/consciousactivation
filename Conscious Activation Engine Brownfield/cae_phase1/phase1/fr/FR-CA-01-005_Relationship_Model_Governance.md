# FR-CA-01-005 — Relationship Model Governance

## Requirement
Meaningful interactions between objects SHALL be represented as typed relationships rather than buried in free-form metadata.

## Required Relation Properties
Subject, object, relation type, direction, temporal semantics where applicable, provenance, confidence, and constraints.

## Examples
- Guest ↔ Audience
- GuestExperiencedTension
- PrimitiveSynergizesWithPrimitive
- SceneExpressesSemanticRole
- CoalitionProducesEdge
- EdgeRoutesToArchetype

## Acceptance Criteria
A relation must answer what it asserts, why the relationship exists, what state/time it applies to, and what it does not imply.
