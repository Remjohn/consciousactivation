# FR-CA-01-007 — Evidence and Provenance Model

## Requirement
Immutable evidence SHALL remain separate from interpretation, inference, and derived artifacts.

## Evidence Classes
- human response / transcript
- research source / citation
- director note
- original visual specimen
- source media
- measurement observation

## Acceptance Criteria
- Evidence has origin, acquisition context, timestamp, and integrity reference where applicable.
- Derived claims link to their evidence.
- Evidence is append-only or immutable by policy.
- Scientific rationale carries epistemic status (`established | supported | proposed | hypothesis`).
