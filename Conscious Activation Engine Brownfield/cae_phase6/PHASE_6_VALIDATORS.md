# Phase 6 Validators

## Core validators

### V01 Evidence Lineage
Every candidate and coalition traces to authenticated evidence.

### V02 Registry Validity
Every activated primitive exists in a canonical registry version.

### V03 Geometry Range
Every primitive weight is inside its admissible geometry.

### V04 Candidate Specificity
Candidate is grounded in the current evidence and is not generic restatement.

### V05 Compatibility
No unapproved antagonistic combination or hidden cancellation.

### V06 Sparsity
Reject ornamental primitive stacking.

### V07 Routeability
Coalition has at least one valid downstream semantic route.

### V08 Invariant Integrity
Active invariant field is preserved.

### V09 Directional Integrity
Representation geometry is not silently distorted.

### V10 Hard Negative
Known deceptive near-neighbors are not reproduced.

### V11 Anti-Centroid
No flattening toward bland consensus or generic motivational language.

### V12 Edge Distinctiveness
Edge Product differs materially from nearest candidate edges.

## Validation outcomes

```text
PASS
PASS_WITH_REVIEW
REPAIR
REJECT
ESCALATE
```

Validators should emit typed reasons, not generic “failed” strings.
