# Conscious Activation Engine — Phase 6 Bundle
## Semantic Candidate Compilation, Coalition Formation & Edge Products

**Phase:** 6  
**Boundary:** Authenticated human evidence → executable semantic force  
**Status:** Draft Source of Truth  
**Architecture law:** Phase 6 transforms authenticated evidence into admissible meaning candidates, sparse primitive coalitions, and an emergent Edge Product. It does not yet decide archetype, scene, visual syntax, or rendering.

### Governing transition

```text
AuthenticatedEvidence
    ↓
SemanticEvidencePacket
    ↓
Invariant / Geometry interpretation
    ↓
Primitive Candidate Field
    ↓
Candidate Eligibility
    ↓
Candidate Survival
    ↓
Compatibility / Antagonism / Redundancy
    ↓
Coalition Weighting
    ↓
Routeability
    ↓
Coalition Signature
    ↓
Edge Product
    ↓
Phase 7 structural realization
```

### Constitutional laws

1. **Authenticated-Evidence Dependency:** Phase 6 may reason over hypotheses, but no candidate may become executable force without traceable evidence lineage.
2. **Large Registry / Small Active Set:** access to many primitives does not authorize activating many primitives.
3. **Candidate Before Coalition:** a primitive family is not a coalition member until an evidence-specific candidate survives.
4. **Coalition Before Edge:** an Edge Product is emergent; it is not a synonym for a primitive, topic, hook, or opinion.
5. **Meaning Before Realization:** Phase 6 does not choose the final archetype, scene, edit, visual syntax, or soundtrack.
6. **Anti-Centroid Enforcement:** generic flattening is a compiler failure, not a preferred safety fallback.
7. **No Unauthorized Softening:** the system MUST NOT remove meaningful tension merely because it is unusual, emotionally charged, controversial, asymmetric, or difficult to categorize.
8. **Evidence / Interpretation Separation:** Guest evidence remains source truth; derived semantic interpretation is separately typed and traceable.
9. **Deterministic Selection:** probabilistic exploration is allowed; coalition admission is governed by explicit schemas, ranges, compatibility rules, validators, and receipts.
10. **Fail Closed, Not Bland:** when semantic force cannot be validated, the system records a typed failure rather than silently producing generic content.

### Phase 6 is not

- a content-writing phase
- an archetype selector
- an editing phase
- a visual composition engine
- an SFL rendering phase
- a substitute for human authentication

### Phase 6 produces

These are the objects downstream systems can query instead of asking agents to rediscover the semantic logic from prose.
