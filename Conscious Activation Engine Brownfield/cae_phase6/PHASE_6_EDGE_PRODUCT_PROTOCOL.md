# Phase 6 Edge Product Protocol

## Definition

The Edge Product is the derived semantic tension object produced when an authenticated evidence field is transformed by a surviving primitive coalition into an executable, audience-relevant pressure.

## Edge formation

An Edge Product should identify:
- the meaningful boundary;
- what expectation is violated, what costly truth is exposed, or what latent pattern is articulated;
- the audience/Guest structural crossing;
- active invariants;
- coalition lineage;
- intended route;
- known failure modes.

## Edge types

The registry may contain maintained edge-type definitions such as:
- recognition_edge
- mechanism_reveal_edge
- authority_sharpening_edge
- empathy_rotation_edge
- identity_contradiction_edge
- expectation_reversal_edge
- status_exposure_edge
- transformation_pressure_edge

These are labels for reusable derived classes, not substitutes for the actual edge description.

## Edge states

```text
hypothesized
→ derived
→ validated
→ routeable
→ executed
→ benchmarked
```

Dynamic outcomes may produce:

```text
weakened
saturated
superseded
fatal
```

## Anti-centroid law

The system must prefer the sharpest evidence-grounded edge over a more generic but safer abstraction, unless a specific downstream contract explicitly requires compression.

## Edge ≠ hook

A hook may later be one expression of an Edge Product.
The Edge Product itself exists before the format and remains portable across downstream realizations.
