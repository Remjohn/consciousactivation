# Evidence Definition Grammar Protocol

## Artifact class

`IMMUTABLE_EVIDENCE`

## Purpose

Defines source-grounded material that supports observation, inference, semantic interpretation, validation, or authorization.

## Definition grammar

Use:

**Source + Observation + Provenance + Scope + Reliability/Evidence Status + Preservation Rule + Interpretation Boundary**

## Definition must establish

- what the source is
- what was actually observed or captured
- where it came from
- when it was captured
- scope and limitations
- evidence status
- what may be inferred and what may not

## Evidence status vocabulary

- `ESTABLISHED`
- `SUPPORTED`
- `PROPOSED`
- `HYPOTHESIS`
- `UNVERIFIED`
- `CONTRADICTED`

## Constitutional law

**Evidence MUST NOT be silently overwritten by interpretation.**

## Examples

`InterviewTranscript`, `ResearchSource`, `DirectorNote`, `OriginalVisualSpecimen`, `OriginalMedia`.

## Hard negatives

- hypothesis presented as evidence
- inferred audience attribute presented as observed fact
- transformed media presented as original media
- citation preserved without source identity
