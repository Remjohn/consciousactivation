# CAE Governance & Specification Bridge Bundle v2.0 — Manifest

## Why v2.0 exists

Version 1.0 established the bridge from CAE Phase 0–7 architecture to brownfield Functional Requirements and Tech Specs.

Version 2.0 adds a missing proof layer: **Reality-Contact Evaluation**.

The system must now distinguish:

- structural test success;
- environment fidelity;
- reward-hacking resistance;
- taste / anti-centroid integrity;
- runtime evidence;
- real-world outcome evidence.

## Core laws introduced in v2.0

### Law 1 — A Green Test Is Not Proof of Quality
A test proves only the proposition it measures.

### Law 2 — Fidelity Must Match the Claim
E0/E1 evidence cannot silently prove E2/E3/E4 claims.

### Law 3 — Every Material Proxy Is Potentially Gameable
Every material evaluator requires a false-proof or reward-hacking counter-test.

### Law 4 — Taste Is an Architecture Property
Taste-sensitive claims require contrastive evidence for edge, specificity, recognition, anti-centroid behavior, and perceptual aliveness.

### Law 5 — Evaluators Are Governed Assets
Validators, scoring functions, fixtures, hard negatives, and mutation suites are themselves versioned and traceable.

### Law 6 — Reality Contact Outranks Proxy Satisfaction
When a proxy score and the underlying observed property disagree, the observed property controls the promotion decision and triggers evaluator review.
