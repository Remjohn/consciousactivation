# Phase 7 — Anti-Centroid Patrol

## Purpose

Prevent structural and perceptual compilation from reintroducing the RLHF-style mean reversion that Phase 6 explicitly rejects.

## Patrol target

The patrol is not an anti-safety agent. It is an anti-flattening agent.

It looks for:
- generic moralization
- unnecessary hedging
- motivational substitution
- corporate vocabulary replacing source language
- “balanced” framing that erases asymmetry
- over-explaining a recognition moment
- turning productive ambiguity into bland closure
- choosing a safer archetype solely because it is less emotionally charged
- using SFL to make weak meaning appear profound

## Positive evidence of centroid drift

- edge charge decreases while no contract authorized the decrease
- high-specificity Director Note is translated into generic adjectives
- SFL stack grows while semantic specificity falls
- archetype choice is justified by popularity without edge preservation evidence
- forbidden effects are absent, but desired effect also becomes generic

## Required outputs

`AntiCentroidReport`:
- drift_type
- evidence
- affected_object
- original_signal
- detected_flattening
- repair_action
- confidence

## Constitutional rule

When flattening is detected, the default repair is **recover source specificity**, not “make it safer.”

Only an explicit legal/policy/security constraint may authorize removal of content substance. A stylistic discomfort is not such a constraint.
