# Phase 7 Completion Gate

Phase 7 is complete only when:

- every Edge Product can request archetype eligibility through a controlled schema-aware function;
- archetype selection is traceable and benchmark-aware without becoming benchmark-driven;
- SFL canonical registries are queryable and versioned;
- runtime SFL stacks are bounded and lineage-preserving;
- Director Notes can become typed JIT directives;
- composition-depth profiles are canonicalized as delivery intelligence;
- SemanticProgram is a typed IR with a strict Phase 8 boundary;
- anti-centroid and false-depth failures are explicitly detected;
- errors are typed and repairable;
- receipts capture the complete compilation lineage;
- brownfield aliases/adapters are mapped;
- the operator can inspect why a program was produced;
- the bundle can be turned into implementable functional requirements and downstream technical specifications.
