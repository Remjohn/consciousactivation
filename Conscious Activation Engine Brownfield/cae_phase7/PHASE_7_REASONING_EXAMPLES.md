# Phase 7 Reasoning Examples

## Example 1 — Recognition Edge

**Input Edge:** a highly specific recognition edge about realizing that “being useful” became the identity criterion.

**Reasoning result:** choose a structure that preserves recognition rather than turning immediately into generic advice. A direct teaching archetype may be rejected if it forces explicit lesson statements that violate the edge's desired ambiguity.

**SFL:** use layered interpretation + quiet repetition + pause weight.

**Depth:** `LayeredInterpretation`.

**Program directive:** allow the audience to complete the recognition themselves.

## Example 2 — High-arousal audience

Same Edge, different state.

The Edge remains the same. The entry architecture can change because media motive and affective state affect receptivity. The system may route the semantic force through a lighter structural carrier while preserving the core edge in the resolution.

```text
same semantic force
```
