# Relational Intelligence Principles

## 1. Relation-before-averaging
A conflict between two valid representations is information. Do not erase it by averaging.

## 2. Evidence-before-inference
Relations can be inferred, but inference must be labeled and linked to evidence.

## 3. Dimension-before-score
A scalar is never a substitute for the dimensions that produced it.

## 4. Historical-before-current
A historical relation must not silently become a current state.

## 5. Adjacency-before-congruence
One matching dimension is not enough to declare deep congruence.

## 6. Human-response supremacy
Relational intelligence can formulate hypotheses; authenticated human response establishes what is actually true for the Guest.

## 7. Anti-centroid preservation
High-charge or unusual relational evidence must not be normalized into generic compatibility language.

## 8. Role-before-schema
Every relational artifact is classified by role before its schema is fixed.

## 9. Query-before-context-dump
Agents should retrieve only the semantic slices needed for the active reasoning plan.

## 10. Provenance survives compression
A compressed relational result must retain lineage to the source objects and evidence.
