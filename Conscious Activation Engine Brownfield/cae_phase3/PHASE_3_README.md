# Conscious Activation Engine — Phase 3 Bundle

## Phase
Phase 3 — Relational Intelligence, Congruence & Context Reasoning

## Purpose
Phase 3 defines the relational intelligence layer that sits between World Intelligence and the downstream activation/semantic compilation stack.

It answers a different question from Phase 2:

> Phase 2 asks: **What exists in the Audience World, Guest World and Cultural World, and what states are active?**
>
> Phase 3 asks: **Where do those worlds structurally intersect, how strong is the intersection, what is merely adjacent, and what reasoning is justified from that relationship?**

## Core architectural position

```text
WORLD INTELLIGENCE
        ↓
RELATIONAL INTELLIGENCE
        ↓
CONGRUENCE / RESONANCE
        ↓
CONTEXT REASONING
        ↓
PRESSURE FIELD CANDIDATES
        ↓
MATRIX OF EDGING
```

Phase 3 does **not** perform final provocation writing, primitive coalition formation, or CMF realization. It produces a typed relational substrate that those later layers are entitled to consume.

## Constitutional distinction

A relationship is evidence about how two or more objects connect. It is not a replacement for either object.

A relation must preserve:

- directionality
- temporal scope
- evidence lineage
- confidence
- derivation method
- current state
- version

## Anti-centroid doctrine

Relational Intelligence MUST NOT collapse distinct structures into a safe average merely because they are difficult to reconcile.

The engine must preserve productive asymmetry, contradiction, and high-signal tension. It may classify an intersection as weak, adjacent, unresolved, or unsupported, but it MUST NOT soften a strong relation into generic compatibility language merely to reduce uncertainty.

## Human-first doctrine

Relational Intelligence earns the right to formulate an activation hypothesis; the interview earns the right to establish human truth.

```text
WORLD MODEL
  → RELATIONAL HYPOTHESIS
  → PROVOCATION
  → HUMAN RESPONSE
  → AUTHENTICATED EVIDENCE
```

## Phase gate

Phase 3 is complete when:

1. Guest ↔ Audience and related cross-domain relationships are explicitly modeled.
2. Congruence is decomposed into typed dimensions rather than one opaque score.
3. Context Reasoning can produce a reproducible, inspectable plan from authorized retrieval.
4. Matrix of Edging can consume a pressure field without inventing upstream facts.
5. Every relational conclusion is traceable to evidence or clearly marked inference.
6. Structured error taxonomy and anti-centroid patrol are active.
7. The system can explain why a relational candidate was selected, rejected, downgraded, or retained.
