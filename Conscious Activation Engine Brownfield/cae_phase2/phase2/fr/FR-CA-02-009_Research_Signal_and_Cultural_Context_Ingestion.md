# FR-CA-02-009 — Research Signal and Cultural Context Ingestion

## Requirement
ResearchSignal and CulturalContext ingestion MUST preserve external evidence as immutable source records with provenance.

## Constraints
Signals should be normalized, tagged, and linked to scope and temporal relevance without prematurely becoming canonical claims.

## Implementation Principle
Cultural memory and current signals remain queryable as source context for later reasoning.

## Acceptance Criteria
- Canonical identity/ownership is explicit.
- Object Constitution fields are defined or marked pending.
- Provenance and lifecycle are testable.
- No silent flattening of evidence into unsupported canonical claims.
- Brownfield status is recorded before replacement.
