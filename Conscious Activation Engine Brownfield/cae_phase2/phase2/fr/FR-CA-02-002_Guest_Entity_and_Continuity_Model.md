# FR-CA-02-002 — Guest Entity and Continuity Model

## Requirement
Guest MUST exist as a persistent canonical person/entity representing the human whose lived expression is being modeled.

## Constraints
Guest identity MUST remain distinct from GuestState, VoiceDNA, StoryArchive, and relational congruence artifacts.

## Implementation Principle
Historical guest evidence and current state must remain queryable without overwriting prior observations.

## Acceptance Criteria
- Canonical identity/ownership is explicit.
- Object Constitution fields are defined or marked pending.
- Provenance and lifecycle are testable.
- No silent flattening of evidence into unsupported canonical claims.
- Brownfield status is recorded before replacement.
