# World Intelligence Governing Principles

1. Audience and Guest are persistent entities, not profiles frozen in prose.
2. Context Premise is evidence-bearing, not the full ontology.
3. State is temporal; history is never silently overwritten.
4. Semantic tension and affective mood are separate dimensions.
5. Media motive is a contextual use condition, not an identity label.
6. A webhook/tension is derived activation pressure, not a topic or social-media hook.
7. A single observation may generate a hypothesis; corroboration is required for promotion according to policy.
8. Original language may be preserved as evidence; canonical semantics remain typed and addressable.
9. Immutable evidence is never rewritten into a more convenient narrative.
10. Derived artifacts must retain lineage to the evidence and rules that produced them.
11. Agents access the world model through authorized semantic functions and query plans.
12. Anti-centroid enforcement protects signal density and native specificity; it does not impose corporate tone or generic safety prose.
