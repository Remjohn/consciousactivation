# Conscious Activation Engine — Research Library v1.0

**Purpose:** Controlled research corpus for reconstructing the CAE Product Brief, PRD, Architecture, Epics, Stories, and later Sessions/Workflows using BMAD.

**Selection rule:** Include artifacts that materially shaped CAE, its CCP/CCF/CMF lineage, its product concepts, its intelligence model, its execution model, or the engineering methodology used to build it.

**Relevance score:** 100 = indispensable; 90–99 = major product/architecture lineage; 80–89 = major supporting source; 70–79 = important supporting context; 60–69 = specialist/context. Scores are research-priority scores, not authority scores.

**Authority note:** `CURRENT` = current repository artifact; `HISTORICAL` = brownfield/archive/source artifact; `TRANSCRIPT` = decision-history record; `REFERENCE` = external methodology/inspiration. Authority must be established during research and not inferred from score.
