# 5. The CAE Causal Operating Model

## 5.1 The Master Transformation

CAE is best understood as a causal operating system rather than a linear sequence of AI calls.

Its canonical transformation is:

**Audience Context → Research & Evidence → Subject Baseline → Narrative Architecture → Declarative PreProduction → Structured Elicitation → Evidence Capture → Collision Analysis → Canonicalization → Composition → AIR Rendering → Human Authorization → Release Manifest → External Distribution → Outcome Measurement → Memory Write-back**



Each stage exists because it establishes conditions required by the next stage. A downstream component may detect or expose an upstream failure, but it cannot always legitimately repair it.

Bad audience framing cannot be fixed by rendering.

Weak evidence cannot be fixed by captions.

Missing Subject authority cannot be fixed by a better model.

An interview that failed to elicit the required evidence cannot be repaired by inventing dialogue.



## 5.2 Stage 1 — Audience Context

**Input:** audience definition, observed tensions, current beliefs, awareness state, prior solution attempts, and intended activation objective.

**Transformation:** identify the interpretive environment in which the Leader's evidence must become meaningful.

**Output:** governed audience context and activation hypotheses.

**Authority:** the Operator and approved research establish the context; models may analyze and recommend but cannot silently redefine the commercial audience.

Audience context is first because the same expertise can matter differently to different audiences. 

## 5.3 Stage 2 — Research & Evidence

**Input:** external research, supplied documents, public sources, campaign context, and prior evidence.

**Transformation:** establish the factual and contextual substrate for narrative planning.

**Output:** research packages, evidence candidates, source references, and context signals.

**Authority:** research systems can retrieve and structure information; source provenance and evidence status remain explicit.

Research gives the Operator and planning system a reference frame against which claims, questions, and narrative opportunities can be evaluated.

## 5.4 Stage 3 — Subject Baseline

**Input:** dedicated Subject elicitation and approved historical evidence.

**Transformation:** establish a governed communication constitution.

**Output:** Baseline Profile, Voice DNA, Emotional DNA, cadence/reaction signals, archetype recommendations, and confirmed boundaries.

**Authority:** the Operator and appropriate human confirmation establish canonical Subject attributes.

This stage prevents the Leader from being treated as an anonymous transcript or arbitrary model inference becoming permanent identity.

## 5.5 Stage 4 — Narrative Architecture

**Input:** audience context, research, Subject Baseline, and Operator intent.

**Transformation:** determine the Core Thesis, narrative arc, tension structure, and format distribution.

**Output:** Narrative Brief and production direction.

**Authority:** models can propose structures; the Operator remains responsible for strategic approval.

Narrative Architecture is where information becomes an intended meaning structure. It does not write the Leader's testimony. It establishes what the interview must discover.

## 5.6 Stage 5 — Declarative PreProduction

**Input:** Narrative Brief, Subject Constitution, audience requirements, and artifact contracts.

**Transformation:** compile narrative intent into an explicit production blueprint.

**Output:** `PreProductionPlan`, including elicitation segments, planned artifacts, format requirements, evidence requirements, alternate angles, and fallback rules.

**Authority:** the Operator authorizes the production plan.

This stage embodies the Blueprint-First principle: Retainer artifacts are known before recording, with each intended artifact tied to an elicitation segment and evidence requirement.

This prevents post-hoc format squeezing.

## 5.7 Stage 6 — Structured Elicitation

**Input:** PreProductionPlan.

**Transformation:** the Operator conducts a human interview designed to elicit required evidence.

**Output:** recorded conversation and source material.

**Authority:** the human Operator controls the live conversation.

CAE does not intervene in real time with generated coaching or prompts. Intelligence operates before capture through planning and after capture through analysis, protecting the human listening relationship.

## 5.8 Stage 7 — Evidence Capture

**Input:** recording, transcript, timestamps, supplied media, and related source material.

**Transformation:** convert raw capture into addressable evidence.

**Output:** transcript segments, source references, candidate moments, timestamps, and provenance.

**Authority:** immutable source evidence remains distinguishable from derived interpretation.

The repository's grammar distinguishes canonical, dynamic, immutable-evidence, and derived objects. Product documentation must preserve that distinction because it determines what later stages may safely change.

## 5.9 Stage 8 — Collision Analysis

**Input:** captured evidence.

**Transformation:** identify collisions, contradictions, relationships, mechanisms, narrative signals, and candidate moments.

**Output:** collision candidates and analysis manifests.

**Authority:** agents recommend; a model finding does not automatically become canonical.

Important meaning can exist at the intersection of statements rather than inside one isolated quote.

## 5.10 Stage 9 — Canonicalization

**Input:** evidence candidates, timestamps, provenance, contradiction relationships, and analysis.

**Transformation:** resolve candidates into governed canonical representations.

**Output:** canonical evidence references for downstream composition.

**Authority:** human governance determines what becomes authoritative where ambiguity matters.

Canonicalization bridges raw evidence and reusable production material while preserving source lineage.

## 5.11 Stage 10 — Composition

**Input:** canonical evidence, narrative intent, artifact contract, and format requirements.

**Transformation:** construct the actual artifact content.

**Output:** video selections, carousel structures, supervisual text, headlines, hooks, and related composition objects.

**Authority:** composition is constrained by artifact-specific policy.

Video dialogue is `VERBATIM_ONLY`.

Permitted derivative lanes may use a tightly limited Voice-DNA bridge, currently governed at a maximum of 20% synthetic character contribution where the product contract permits it.

Editorial headlines and hooks may be transformed without being confused with Subject testimony.

Composition cannot invent upstream meaning.

## 5.12 Stage 11 — AIR Rendering

**Input:** governed composition objects and visual/audio assets.

**Transformation:** render approved semantic objects into production-ready media.

**Output:** rendered assets and technical metadata.

**Authority:** rendering cannot alter semantic meaning.

Rendering can solve timing, layout, encoding, typography, visual assembly, and other realization problems. It cannot solve missing evidence or rewrite the Subject.

## 5.13 Stage 12 — Human Authorization

**Input:** rendered artifacts, provenance, composition metadata, quality metrics, and readiness state.

**Transformation:** determine whether release is authorized.

**Output:** approved, rejected, blocked, or exception-handled state.

**Authority:** human authority is decisive for irreversible decisions.

CAE fails closed: unmet evidence, provenance, composition, authority, or readiness requirements remain visible rather than silently lowering the standard.

## 5.14 Stage 13 — Release Manifest and Distribution

**Input:** authorized assets and release metadata.

**Transformation:** package deterministic information for downstream distribution.

**Output:** `DistributionExportManifest` or equivalent release package.

**Authority:** CAE prepares interoperability but does not autonomously publish, operate communities, buy advertising, or make external distribution decisions.

The principle is:

> **No autonomous distribution. Yes interoperability.**

## 5.15 Stage 14 — Outcome Measurement

**Input:** published artifact outcomes and operational observations.

**Transformation:** measure activation and production performance.

**Output:** outcome records, quality metrics, model telemetry, and learning signals.

Activation should be distinguished from vanity. Attention is useful, but trust, authority, qualified response, conversion pressure, and other meaningful outcomes matter more than raw volume.

Model telemetry should also remain connected to outcomes. CAE needs to know not only which model produced a decision, but whether that decision required correction and whether the resulting artifact performed.

## 5.16 Stage 15 — Memory Write-Back

**Input:** validated outcomes, human corrections, canonical evidence, and production history.

**Transformation:** convert experience into governed reusable knowledge.

**Output:** updated Subject memory, audience learning, narrative patterns, benchmark records, routing evidence, and operational knowledge.

This is where CAE compounds.

A production cycle should make the next cycle better informed without allowing learning to override constitutional governance.

## 5.17 The Causal Law

The operating model can be reduced to one law:

> **Downstream realization cannot legitimately invent upstream meaning.**

Editing cannot create evidence. Captions cannot create lived experience. Rendering cannot create narrative structure. A model cannot authorize itself. A benchmark cannot change a constitutional rule. Outcome data can improve future hypotheses, but it cannot retroactively make an unsupported claim true.

This law turns CAE from a collection of automation components into a governed causal system.

## 5.18 Product-Level View

CAE is a set of authority-bound transformations connected by provenance. Its reliability comes from making every transition explicit:

**What entered? What changed? What artifact was created? Who could decide? What evidence supports the result? What happens if the requirement is not met?**

When those questions can be answered at every stage, CAE becomes observable, testable, and evolvable.

That is the operating model: a controlled causal chain from audience need to human evidence, from evidence to governed expression, and from observed outcome back into better future production.
