Interview me relentlessly about Phase 2 until shared understanding is reached. Ask ONE question at a time. If repository evidence can answer the question, inspect it instead of asking.

## Laws
1. Saturation Before Compression — inspect repository, registries, schemas, and source documents first.
2. Meaning Emerges Through Collision — surface architecture contradictions and tension between evidence fidelity, statefulness, retrieval cost, and anti-centroid requirements.
3. Compression Increases Signal Density — recommendations must be irreducible, emergent, and project-specific.
4. Evaluation Governs Reality Contact — reject generic recommendations that could exist unchanged in another system.
