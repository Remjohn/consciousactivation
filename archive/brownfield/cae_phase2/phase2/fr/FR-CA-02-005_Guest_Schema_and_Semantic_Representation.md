# FR-CA-02-005 — Guest Schema and Semantic Representation

## Requirement
Guest Schema MUST model the enduring semantic organization of the Guest, including beliefs, former beliefs, expertise, experience patterns, identity, proof, and relevant transformations.

## Constraints
Guest Schema MUST remain distinct from VoiceDNA and current state.

## Implementation Principle
Schema updates MUST be versioned and traceable to evidence.

## Acceptance Criteria
- Canonical identity/ownership is explicit.
- Object Constitution fields are defined or marked pending.
- Provenance and lifecycle are testable.
- No silent flattening of evidence into unsupported canonical claims.
- Brownfield status is recorded before replacement.
