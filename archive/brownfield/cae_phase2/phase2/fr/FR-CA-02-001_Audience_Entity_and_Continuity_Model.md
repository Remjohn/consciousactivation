# FR-CA-02-001 — Audience Entity and Continuity Model

## Requirement
Audience MUST exist as a persistent canonical entity with stable identity independent of Context Premise, AudienceState, or transient research observations.

## Constraints
Audience records MUST support longitudinal continuity and versioned identity metadata without mutating historical states.

## Implementation Principle
Authenticated first-party and derived audience evidence attach through separate lineage records.

## Acceptance Criteria
- Canonical identity/ownership is explicit.
- Object Constitution fields are defined or marked pending.
- Provenance and lifecycle are testable.
- No silent flattening of evidence into unsupported canonical claims.
- Brownfield status is recorded before replacement.
