# FR-CA-02-010 — Evidence Corroboration and Signal-vs-Noise Resolution

## Requirement
The engine MUST distinguish isolated observations from corroborated signals before promoting derived state or activation candidates.

## Constraints
Corroboration should support configurable independent-signal thresholds, contradiction tracking, and evidence quality weighting.

## Implementation Principle
The system must preserve uncertainty instead of treating repetition from the same source as independent confirmation.

## Acceptance Criteria
- Canonical identity/ownership is explicit.
- Object Constitution fields are defined or marked pending.
- Provenance and lifecycle are testable.
- No silent flattening of evidence into unsupported canonical claims.
- Brownfield status is recorded before replacement.
