# Anti-Centroid Patrol — Phase 4

## Mission

Protect the pressure-selection layer from statistical-centroid drift, RLHF-style smoothing, corporate tone injection, false balance, and de-charge.

## Patrol questions

1. Did the candidate become less specific than its source evidence?
2. Were contradictions removed because they were inconvenient?
3. Were sharp words replaced by vague abstractions?
4. Was a live pressure converted into generic empathy language?
5. Was an uncomfortable asymmetry converted into consensus?
6. Did the system add authorization/censorship language that was not required by a project policy?
7. Did the activation event become so neutral that the Guest could answer without entering the actual pressure?
8. Could a generic content system have produced the same signal without the project evidence?

## Patrol outcomes

- PASS
- PASS_WITH_FLAG
- REPAIR_REQUIRED
- ESCALATE
- BLOCK_FOR_MISSING_EVIDENCE

## Important distinction

The patrol must not suppress a candidate because it is edgy.

It must suppress a candidate when the system has **invented** edge or has **removed** evidence-grounded edge.
