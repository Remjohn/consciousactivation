# Conscious Activation Engine — Phase 4 Bundle

## Phase
Phase 4 — Pressure Field, Matrix of Edging & Activation Selection

## Purpose
Phase 4 converts the relational substrate from Phase 3 into a governed field of live semantic pressure and selects the **broad primary signal** that is worthy of activating a human interview.

Phase 4 answers:

> **What is sufficiently alive, evidenced, relationally grounded, fresh, and high-signal that we should deliberately place in front of the Guest without pre-writing the Guest's answer?**

## Canonical boundary

```text
WORLD INTELLIGENCE
      ↓
RELATIONAL INTELLIGENCE
      ↓
PRESSURE / TENSION FIELD
      ↓
MATRIX OF EDGING
      ↓
BROAD PRIMARY SIGNAL
      ↓
ACTIVATION / PROVOCATION
      ↓
HUMAN INTERVIEW (Phase 5)
```

Phase 4 does **not** authenticate the Guest's final position. It earns the right to ask. Human response remains the source of authenticated expression.

## Core doctrines

- **Pressure is not a topic.** It is a meaningful unresolved boundary with sufficient magnitude to alter attention, recognition, belief, or decision relevance.
- **Webhook is not a social-media hook.** In CAE it is an activatable tension state/event that can legitimately trigger an upstream activation attempt.
- **The first edge is broad.** The Matrix of Edging must provoke without scripting the answer.
- **The matrix selects pressure, not style.** Editing style, SFL, archetype, scene, and visual syntax are downstream.
- **Anti-centroid is constitutional.** Flattening, premature softening, false consensus, and RLHF-like smoothing are explicit failure modes.
- **Evidence density precedes confidence.** One weak signal is not a state.
- **Contradiction is preserved.** A high-value contradiction may be more useful than a harmonious average.
- **Human-first remains binding.** No relational or edging hypothesis becomes Guest truth until authenticated through the interview.

## Phase gate

Phase 4 is complete when a human operator can inspect a selected BroadPrimarySignal and answer:

1. What pressure is being activated?
2. What evidence makes it live rather than merely imaginable?
3. Which audience/guest/context relationships support it?
4. Which contradictions or asymmetries are preserved?
5. Why did this candidate survive over its nearest alternatives?
6. What is the Matrix of Edging allowed to do to it?
7. What must the provocation avoid saying on the Guest's behalf?
8. What anti-centroid checks ran?
9. What evidence is still hypothetical and therefore must be tested by the interview?
10. What downstream event will update the state after the Guest responds?
