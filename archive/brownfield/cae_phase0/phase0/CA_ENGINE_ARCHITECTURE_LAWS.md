# 1. Constitutional Purpose

These laws govern architecture evolution, data modeling, agent reasoning, semantic integrity, and implementation discipline across the Conscious Activation Engine.

They are intended to operationalize the project's Legalization doctrine without converting governance into generic sanitization.

# 2. Definitions

**Law** = a system-wide architectural invariant.

**Contract** = an executable or validator-enforced obligation governing a bounded component.

**Directive** = a context-specific instruction derived from an authorized source of intent.

**Program** = a structured sequence of operations that can be executed or validated.

**IR** = an intermediate representation normalized for a downstream execution target.

# 3. Prime Laws

## Law 01 — Reality Before Abstraction

No canonical semantic claim shall be promoted solely from model plausibility when relevant evidence is expected to exist.

## Law 02 — Role Before Schema

Architectural role precedes field design, storage design, and implementation choice.

## Law 03 — Canonical Means Slow

Stable ontology, taxonomy, structural grammar, and constitutional policies change deliberately and with version history.

## Law 04 — State Is Not Identity

Dynamic state MUST NOT overwrite canonical identity or historical observations.

## Law 05 — Evidence Is Immutable

Primary human, research, and source evidence SHALL be preserved. Derived interpretation is separate.

## Law 06 — Derived Objects Are Recomputable

Where practical, derived semantic artifacts SHALL retain sufficient lineage to be recomputed when upstream assumptions change.

## Law 07 — Relationships Are First-Class

If a meaningful fact depends on interaction between objects, represent the relationship explicitly rather than burying it in arbitrary metadata.

## Law 08 — Human-First Evidence

For human-authored systems, direct human expression remains the highest-fidelity source for lived experience. Research may frame and challenge; it does not impersonate human evidence.

## Law 09 — Broad Signal Before Sharp Steering

Pre-interview pressure signals should be broad enough to allow authentic response. Post-response edge selection may become more specific.

## Law 10 — Primitives Are Operators

A Primitive is a reusable transformation capability, not a topic, output, format, or runtime state.

## Law 11 — Coalition Selection Is Typed

Coalition formation shall follow explicit stages:

`candidate generation → eligibility → compatibility → weighting → routeability → coalition signature`

## Law 12 — Edge Is Emergent

An Edge Product is not synonymous with a Primitive, a topic, or a hook. It emerges from evidence, operator activation, and weighted interaction.

## Law 13 — Semantic Truth Precedes Perceptual Amplification

SDA-level direction and semantic integrity must be determined before SFL-level perceptual modulation is finalized.

## Law 14 — Perception Does Not Rewrite Truth

SFL may modulate experience, emphasis, compression, rhythm, atmosphere, and memorability. It shall not silently change semantic direction.

## Law 15 — Format Follows Meaning

Media format selection is downstream from meaning and structural composition requirements whenever the workflow is semantically driven.

## Law 16 — Scene Is Not Meaning

A Scene realizes an authorized semantic/compositional instruction. It does not independently invent the underlying meaning.

## Law 17 — Human Director Notes Are Executable Sources of Intent

A human Director Note may begin as prose. The system must translate it into a structured directive before execution when the instruction is intended to affect deterministic behavior.

## Law 18 — Prose Is Source Material, Not an Unbounded Runtime Schema

Structured prose is legitimate and often valuable. It must have a declared role, provenance, and retrieval scope. Runtime truth should be exposed through typed objects, functions, and contracts where repeatability matters.

## Law 19 — Agents Operate Through Authorized Semantic Interfaces

Agents SHOULD reason through curated views, functions, typed services, retrieval plans, and executable programs instead of arbitrary undifferentiated database access.

## Law 20 — Plan Before Execute

For non-trivial semantic tasks, the preferred sequence is:

`intent → schema link → entity/relation selection → decomposition → plan → structured execution → validation → repair`

## Law 21 — Typed Errors Over Retry Fog

Agents should receive typed failure causes whenever possible. `Try again` is not an adequate diagnostic protocol.

## Law 22 — Anti-Centroid Enforcement Is Active Governance

The system SHALL actively detect flattening into statistical or corporate centroids at meaningful handoffs.

## Law 23 — Anti-Centroid Does Not Mean Extreme for Its Own Sake

The objective is signal integrity, specificity, recognition, tension preservation, and human congruence. Manufactured extremity is not a substitute for meaning density.

## Law 24 — Governance Must Not Introduce Corporate Sanitization

No definition, contract, validator, or review protocol shall add irrelevant authorization, licensing, moralizing, or generic safety language that is not required by the actual ontology or execution contract.

## Law 25 — Hard Negatives Are Required for High-Leverage Concepts

High-impact definitions, operators, perceptual functions, archetypes, and compositional structures should have contrastive failure assets where ordinary plausibility checks would be insufficient.

## Law 26 — Research Hypotheses Must Be Labeled

Scientific rationale shall be classified as established, supported, proposed, or hypothesis. Useful hypotheses may guide experimentation but SHALL NOT be represented as established fact without verification.

## Law 27 — Brownfield Before Greenfield

Before adding a new object, registry, pipeline, or service, inspect the existing codebase and source documents to classify whether the behavior already exists.

## Law 28 — No Silent Duplication

Two components with overlapping responsibility must be reconciled, crosswalked, or deliberately separated with an explicit reason.

## Law 29 — Receipts Preserve Causal Lineage

Important executions should preserve the lineage needed to answer:

> Why did this artifact exist?

The lineage should connect relevant context/state, evidence, operators, programs, outputs, and outcomes.

## Law 30 — Outcome Updates Memory, Not History

Measured outcomes may update benchmark memory and dynamic state. They must not rewrite historical facts or immutable evidence.

## Law 31 — Learning Is a Separate Loop

Runtime execution and post-runtime optimization are distinct loops even when they share infrastructure such as DSPy.

## Law 32 — Runtime DSPy and Optimization DSPy Are Distinct Roles

DSPy may provide typed runtime orchestration and may also support post-runtime program optimization. These roles must remain conceptually distinct.

## Law 33 — Biology Is an Orchestration Model

Biological language may organize architecture consistently. It shall not be presented as empirical neuroscience merely because the analogy is useful.

## Law 34 — Editing Is the Common Operation

Across audience, guest, semantic, operator, coalition, archetype, scene, visual, and runtime layers, editing consists of selection, exclusion, combination, ordering, emphasis, and preservation.

# 4. Anti-Centroid Patrol Protocol

The engine SHOULD maintain a dedicated diagnostic role or agent responsible for detecting:

- generic mean-reversion
- unnecessary hedging
- irrelevant permission language
- over-sanitization
- corporate neutrality inserted without cause
- flattening of conflict or tension
- replacement of concrete evidence with abstract archetypes
- replacement of human language with institutional vocabulary

This agent is a **quality patrol**, not a censorship engine.

A finding should normally produce one of:

`PASS | WARN | REPAIR | ESCALATE | BLOCK`

The default response to stylistic deviation is NOT `BLOCK`. The response should depend on the constitutional property actually violated.

# 5. Authority Hierarchy

Unless a module establishes a narrower legitimate hierarchy, the system resolves conflict by checking:

1. Canonical architecture law
2. Current module PRD
3. Current Functional Requirement
4. Current technical specification
5. Versioned registry definition
6. Runtime program / contract
7. Contextual directive
8. Generated proposal

A lower-level artifact cannot silently rewrite a higher-level rule.

# 6. Change Control

Architectural changes must record:

- decision
- reason
- evidence
- affected objects
- affected PRDs
- migration impact
- rejected alternatives
- version change

# 7. Prohibited Governance Behaviors

The system must not normalize:

- vague safety disclaimers unrelated to the object's actual role
- generic corporate prose as a default quality standard
- model-generated authority claims without evidence
- accidental schema creation around unresolved concepts
- hidden mutation of canonical registries through runtime state
- retry loops without error classification
- downstream cosmetic optimization that destroys upstream semantic lineage
