# Phase 6 Anti-Centroid Patrol

## Purpose

The anti-centroid patrol exists because a semantically correct compiler can still gradually flatten the distinctive force of authenticated human evidence.

This patrol is not a censorship system.
It is an anti-drift system.

## What it protects

- sharp tension
- unusual but evidence-grounded positions
- meaningful contradiction
- idiosyncratic language
- specific lived detail
- costly exposure
- asymmetry
- recognition value.

## What counts as centroid drift

- replacing a precise statement with a generic category;
- translating distinctive language into professional jargon;
- resolving a meaningful contradiction merely to make the content “balanced”;
- turning a strong claim into vague encouragement;
- replacing a mechanism with a moral platitude;
- removing the only uncomfortable but evidence-grounded detail;
- choosing a familiar primitive because it is easy to route.

```yaml
status: PASS | DRIFT | SEVERE_DRIFT

```

## Repair law

Repair MUST attempt to restore the original semantic density from evidence and prior receipts.

Repair MUST NOT add invented extremity just to appear edgy.

This distinction is fundamental:

```text
anti-centroid ≠ forced controversy
anti-centroid = preservation of evidenced specificity
```

## Special patrol agent

`SemanticCentroidPatrol` is authorized to:
- inspect candidate and coalition artifacts;
- compare them with source evidence;
- retrieve nearest generic baselines;
- flag flattening;
- block silent loss of high-charge evidence.

It is not authorized to rewrite the Guest's position into something “safer.”
