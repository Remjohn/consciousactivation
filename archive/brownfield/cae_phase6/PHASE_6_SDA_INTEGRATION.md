# Phase 6 SDA Integration

## SDA is upstream semantic substrate

Phase 6 does not redefine SDA objects.

It consumes:
- Existential Invariant
- Representation Geometry
- Archetypal Geometry
- Species Composition Grammar
- contextual invariant signals where available
- hard negatives and directional policies where required

## Runtime chain

```text
AuthenticatedEvidence
→ InvariantFieldPacket
→ Geometry Packets
→ PrimitiveCandidate Field
→ Coalition
→ EdgeProduct
```

## Important distinctions

### Invariant Gravity
Canonical property of an existential invariant.

### Invariant Activation Intensity
Runtime strength of activation in current evidence/context.

### Invariant Resonance Multiplier
Runtime composition effect produced by invariant + coalition + geometry + context.

Phase 6 must not collapse these into one “importance” score.

## Directional integrity

A coalition may be locally compelling and still violate the target representation geometry. Such coalitions must fail directional validation rather than being silently softened.

## Hard negatives

Hard negatives should be retrieved before final coalition commitment when a candidate sits near a known semantic corruption boundary.

The hard negative test is contrastive:
- positive anchor;
- near-neighbor failure;
- divergence axis;
- expected validator response.
