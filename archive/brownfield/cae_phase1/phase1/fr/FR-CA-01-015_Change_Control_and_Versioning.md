# FR-CA-01-015 — Architecture Change Control and Versioning

## Requirement
Canonical architecture, taxonomy, schemas, registries, contracts, and class-specific definition grammars SHALL be versioned and changed through explicit decisions.

## Required Change Record
- decision
- rationale
- evidence
- collision/tension addressed
- objects affected
- PRDs/FRs affected
- migration impact
- rejected alternatives
- version change
- regression/evaluation requirements

## Acceptance Criteria
A canonical change without source lineage and migration impact cannot be promoted to production architecture.
