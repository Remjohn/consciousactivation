# Conscious Activation Engine — Phase 5 Bundle

## Phase
Phase 5 — Human Interview, Authentication & Evidence Acquisition

## Purpose
Phase 5 is the human-first truth-acquisition boundary of CAE. It receives a governed activation event from Phase 4 and converts the Guest's voluntary response into immutable, traceable evidence that downstream semantic compilation may legally and structurally use.

## Core constitutional laws

1. **The system earns the right to ask; the Guest earns the right to define the answer.**
2. **The Guest response is evidence; the Phase 4 hypothesis is not evidence of the Guest's position.**
3. **Questions may expose pressure, but may not smuggle in a conclusion.**
4. **Human language is preserved before abstraction.**
5. **Immutable evidence is never overwritten by a later interpretation.**
6. **Derived interpretations must retain explicit lineage to the original response.**
7. **Uncertainty is represented explicitly; agents do not convert uncertainty into confidence by prose.**
8. **The Anti-Centroid Patrol protects the Guest's specificity from being normalized into safe, generic language.**
9. **The interview is a thinking instrument as well as an extraction instrument: questions may help the Guest articulate what is already present, but must not manufacture beliefs.**
10. **No downstream content compilation may treat an unauthenticated hypothesis as Guest truth.**

## Phase gate

Phase 5 is complete when every downstream-eligible semantic claim has:

- a source response,
- immutable evidence lineage,
- a clear distinction between verbatim evidence and interpretation,
- an authentication status,
- an uncertainty/confidence record,
- an explanation of how the question that elicited it was constructed,
- and a receipt that permits reconstruction of the path from Phase 4 activation to Phase 5 evidence.

## Explicit non-goals

Phase 5 does not:

- choose the final primitive coalition,
- write the final script,
- select the final media format,
- invent missing Guest positions,
- silently rewrite evidence into polished marketing language,
- or convert a plausible answer into a canonical belief without evidence.
