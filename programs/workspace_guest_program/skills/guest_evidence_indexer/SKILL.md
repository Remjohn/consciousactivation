# Guest Evidence Indexer Skill

## Purpose
Indexes and records cryptographic SHA-256 fingerprints of source recordings, transcripts, and consent records.

## Rules
- Flat skill only: no sub-skills or subagent delegation.
- Protected source-bearing evidence cannot be modified or overwritten.
