# Worked Example — Hypothesis to Question

## Candidate collision

Audience cognitive island: prestige is treated as identity.

Guest territory: Guest left an apparently successful career after a period of burnout.

World signal: current public discussion about burnout and professional identity.

Edge: career prestige may preserve an identity that no longer fits lived reality.

Archetype opportunity: transformation story / myth break.

Desired evidence: specific decision, consequence, and changed operating standard.

## Hypothesis

The Guest's current philosophy contradicts their former operating model, and the contradiction may reveal a non-obvious transformation mechanism relevant to the audience's current state.

## Question coalition

`Past-Self Contrast + Contradiction Repair + Answer Resolution Escalation`

## Initial Question IR

Objective: recover the moment of contradiction.

Target: one psychological decision point.

Expected response: episode → decision → consequence.

## Natural-language realization

“You used to make the kind of decision you're now warning people about. What happened that made you change your mind?”

## Routing examples

- generic answer → specificity escalation;
- broad explanation → descriptive-before-explanatory;
- concrete episode → expand sequence/consequence;
- contradiction → reconciliation;
- novel interpretation → interpret-and-return;
- missing evidence → evidence challenge.

The example illustrates the architecture; it does not declare these mechanism names canonical.
