## 4. Explicit Non-Goals & Invariants

1. **No Autonomous Broadcast Without Authority:** Under no policy configuration may the engine publish content to public external destinations without an authenticated, signed `AuthorizationDecisionReceipt` or `ProductionReleaseSeal`.
2. **No Unanchored Semantic Invention:** Creative composition engines are strictly forbidden from inventing factual claims or statements ungrounded in sovereign source evidence.
3. **No In-Place Policy Mutation:** Active, in-flight campaign executions retain their bound policy revision digest; policy updates apply strictly prospectively.
4. **No Raw Memory Overwrite:** Outcome measurements cannot directly overwrite canonical memory; insights must earn evidentiary scope as Learning Candidates before promotion.
