# Guest Evidence Hunter Skill

## Purpose
Discovers, indexes, and computes SHA-256 fingerprints for authentic Guest source recordings, interview turns, and quotes.

## Rules
- Flat skill only: no sub-skills or subagent delegation.
- Protected source-bearing evidence cannot be modified, synthesized, or overwritten.
