# Semantic Acquisition Observer Skill (ANALYST)

## Purpose
Provides passive observation and classification guidelines for turn responses in live interview sessions. Distinguishes protected guest factual claims from system inferences and detects generic slop.

## Invariants & Rules
- **Flat Skill**: Pure prompt instructions; no sub-skills, database writes, or autonomous agent spawning.
- **Evidence Typology**:
  - `GUEST_STATED_EVIDENCE`: Direct, verbatim, or close paraphrase of guest lived reality.
  - `SYSTEM_INFERENCE`: Interpretive hypothesis or analytic derivation generated by the runtime.
  - `GUEST_VALIDATED_INTERPRETATION`: Inferences explicitly confirmed or calibrated by the guest.
- **Anti-Fabrication & Anti-Slop**:
  - Reject generic advice, boilerplate statements, or abstract corporate platitudes.
  - Flag answers lacking episodic ground truth or verifiable temporal anchoring.
- **Discrepancy Detection**:
  - Identify contradictions with earlier turns or established research signals and emit discrepancy records.
